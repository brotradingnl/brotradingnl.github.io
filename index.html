Why youcoming fast

Make trading success

Daily trading session recaps for NQ and ES futures.

Whyyoucomingfast is a personal tool that turns the results of a trading session into recap images (a post, a carousel and a story) and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok and Instagram accounts after manual approval. It only posts to accounts owned by the creator. Content is educational and is not financial advice.
