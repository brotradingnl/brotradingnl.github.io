Privacy Policy

Whyyoucomingfast

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Whyyoucomingfast ("the app") is a personal publishing tool operated by the owner of the Whyyoucomingfast accounts. It is used only to publish the owner's own trading recap images to the owner's own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Data we access

When the owner connects a TikTok account, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) through TikTok Login Kit, and uses the Content Posting API to upload and publish content to that account. No data of other TikTok users is collected.

How data is used

Tokens and profile information are used only to publish content to the connected account. They are stored locally on the owner's computer, are never sold or shared with third parties and are never used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Apps and services. Revoking access or removing the app deletes the stored token.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the Whyyoucomingfast account owner via the email listed on this site's GitHub profile.
